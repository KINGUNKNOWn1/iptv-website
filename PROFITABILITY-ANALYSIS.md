## 🎯 Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

### Average Customer Value
**Assumptions:**
- 60% churn after 1 month
- 40% stay 6+ months
- 20% stay 12+ months

**Calculation:**
```
Month 1: €7.67 (100% of customers)
Month 2: €7.67 (40% renew) = €3.07
Month 3: €7.67 (35% remain) = €2.68
Month 6: €7.67 (25% remain) = €1.92
Month 12: €7.67 (20% remain) = €1.53

Average LTV = €16.87 per customer
Average Profit = €16.87 - (€1.11 × 6 months avg) = €10.21
```

### LTV to CAC Ratio
**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) with ads:**
- €300/month ÷ 27 customers = €11.11/customer

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
- LTV: €10.21
- CAC: €11.11
- **Ratio: 0.92:1** (Unprofitable in short term)

**TO BE PROFITABLE, YOU NEED:**
- LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1 or higher
- Either: Reduce CAC to €3.40/customer (impossible with ads)
- Or: Increase LTV to €33.33+ (need better retention)

**CONCLUSION:** Paid ads are NOT profitable unless:
1. You get 80%+ retention (requires amazing service)
2. You upsell customers to higher tiers
3. You use free marketing to reduce CAC to near €0
