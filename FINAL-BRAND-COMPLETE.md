## 🛡️ Brand Guidelines

### Logo Do's:
✅ Use provided SVG files
✅ Maintain minimum size (100px width)
✅ Keep orange dot color (#FF6B2C)
✅ Use on white, dark, or brand colors
✅ Maintain letter spacing

### Logo Don'ts:
❌ Don't stretch or distort
❌ Don't change weights (stream=400, holland=800)
❌ Don't rotate or skew
❌ Don't add effects (shadows, gradients, etc.)
❌ Don't change orange color
