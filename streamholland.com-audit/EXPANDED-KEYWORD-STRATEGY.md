## 💡 Content Writing Tips

### Natural Keyword Integration:
✅ **Good:** "Stream Holland biedt IPTV service in heel Nederland, inclusief Amsterdam, Rotterdam en Den Haag."

❌ **Bad:** "IPTV Holland IPTV Netherlands IPTV Amsterdam IPTV Rotterdam"

### Use Synonyms:
- Holland ↔ Netherlands
- Nederland ↔ Dutch
- Abonnement ↔ Service ↔ Pakket
- Zonder contract ↔ Vrijblijvend ↔ Flexibel

### Answer User Intent:
For "iptv zonder contract":
- ✅ Explain what it means
- ✅ Compare to contracts
- ✅ Clarify no auto-renewal
- ✅ Show pricing transparency
