## 🎯 ONGOING: LINK BUILDING TACTICS (NO PROFILES NEEDED)

### 1. Resource Page Link Building
**What:** Find pages listing "Best IPTV providers" or "Streaming resources"

**How:**
```bash
# Google searches to find resource pages:
"best iptv providers" + "resources"
"iptv nederland" + "links"
"streaming services" + inurl:links
```

**Email template:**
```
Subject: IPTV resource suggestion

Hi,

I noticed your page about [TOPIC] and found it very helpful.

I run Stream Holland (https://iptv-website-phi.vercel.app),
a Dutch IPTV service with 30,500+ channels.

Would you consider adding us to your resources list?

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

### 2. Broken Link Building
**What:** Find broken links on IPTV/streaming websites, offer your link as replacement

**Tool:** Check My Links (Chrome extension) - FREE
**How:**
1. Find IPTV/streaming blogs
2. Use extension to find broken links (404 errors)
3. Email webmaster: "I found a broken link on your page, here's a working alternative"

**Email template:**
```
Subject: Broken link on [PAGE TITLE]

Hi,

I was reading your article "[ARTICLE]" and noticed a broken link in [SECTION].

I have a similar resource that might be a good replacement:
https://iptv-website-phi.vercel.app/[relevant-page]

Hope this helps!

[Your name]
```

### 3. Competitor Backlink Analysis
**What:** See where your competitors get backlinks, get the same

**Free tool:** Ahrefs Backlink Checker - https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker
- Enter competitor URL
- See their backlinks
- Submit your site to the same directories

**Find competitors:**
```
Google: "iptv nederland"
Google: "iptv abonnement"
Google: "iptv kopen"
```
Top 5 results = your competitors. Check their backlinks.
