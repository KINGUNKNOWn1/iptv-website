========================================
EMAIL TIPS
========================================

DO's:
✓ Personalize every email (use their name, reference their content)
✓ Keep it short and to the point (under 200 words)
✓ Provide value first (what's in it for them?)
✓ Be specific about what you want
✓ Include a clear call-to-action
✓ Proofread before sending
✓ Follow up once if no response (after 1 week)

DON'Ts:
✗ Don't use generic templates without personalization
✗ Don't spam or send mass emails
✗ Don't be pushy or desperate
✗ Don't make it all about you
✗ Don't follow up more than twice
✗ Don't send from a free email (Gmail, Hotmail)
✗ Don't include too many links

BEST PRACTICES:
• Send emails on Tuesday-Thursday between 10am-2pm
• Use a professional email signature
• Track opens and clicks (use HubSpot or similar)
• Keep a spreadsheet of all outreach
• Wait 7 days before following up
• Respond within 24 hours when they reply
• Always thank them for their time

TOOLS:
• Hunter.io - Find email addresses
• Mailshake - Email outreach automation
• Grammarly - Grammar checking
• Google Docs - Draft and collaborate
• Streak - Gmail CRM for tracking

========================================
TRACKING YOUR OUTREACH
========================================

Create a spreadsheet with these columns:
• Date Sent
• Recipient Name
• Website/Blog
• Email Address
• Template Used
• Subject Line
• Opened? (Y/N)
• Replied? (Y/N)
• Result (Backlink/No/Pending)
• Follow-up Date
• Notes

This helps you:
- Know who you've contacted
- When to follow up
- What works and what doesn't
- Measure your success rate

========================================
SUCCESS METRICS
========================================

Good outreach response rates:
• 5-10% response rate = Average
• 10-20% response rate = Good
• 20%+ response rate = Excellent

If your response rate is low:
→ Improve personalization
→ Make your value proposition clearer
→ Find better contact information
→ Target more relevant websites
→ Improve your subject lines
