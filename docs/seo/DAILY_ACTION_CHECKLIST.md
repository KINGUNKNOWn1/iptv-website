## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Don't:**
- Submit to spam directories (quality > quantity)
- Send generic emails without personalization
- Drop links in forums immediately (build presence first)
- Ignore tracking (you can't improve what you don't measure)
- Give up after 1 week (SEO takes 3-6 months)

✅ **Do:**
- Focus on quality backlinks
- Personalize every outreach email
- Provide value before asking for links
- Track everything in spreadsheets
- Be consistent (2 hours/week is enough)
