## What Are Backlinks?

A **backlink** is a link from another website to yours. Example:

```
TechBlog.nl writes article → Links to streamholland.nl → That's a backlink!
```

**Why Google Cares:**
- Backlinks are like votes of confidence
- More quality backlinks = higher trust = better rankings
- Think of it as: "If reputable sites link to you, you must be trustworthy"

## Types of Backlinks

### DoFollow vs NoFollow

**DoFollow Backlinks** ✅ (Most Valuable)
```html
<a href="https://streamholland.nl">IPTV Nederland</a>
```
- Passes SEO "link juice"
- Improves rankings
- **PRIORITY:** Get these!

**NoFollow Backlinks** ⚡ (Still Valuable)
```html
<a href="https://streamholland.nl" rel="nofollow">IPTV Nederland</a>
```
- Doesn't pass direct SEO value
- Still drives traffic
- Looks natural to Google
- **Also important!**

### Quality Indicators

**High-Quality Backlink:**
- ✅ From relevant website (tech, streaming, Netherlands)
- ✅ Domain Authority 30+
- ✅ Natural anchor text
- ✅ Contextual (within article content)
- ✅ DoFollow

**Low-Quality Backlink:**
- ❌ Spam website
- ❌ Unrelated industry (e.g., casino, pharma)
- ❌ Link farms
- ❌ Footer/sidebar only
- ❌ Paid link schemes

## Important Warnings

### What NOT to Do

**❌ DON'T Buy Backlinks**
- Google penalizes paid link schemes
- Can get your site banned
- Not worth the risk

**❌ DON'T Use Link Farms**
- Low-quality spammy sites
- Hurts more than helps
- Google will catch you

**❌ DON'T Over-Optimize Anchor Text**
- Vary your anchor text
- Don't always use "IPTV Nederland"
- Mix: branded, naked URL, generic

**❌ DON'T Spam Comments/Forums**
- Quality over quantity
- Adds value first
- Link second

**✅ DO Build Natural, Quality Links**
- Focus on relationships
- Create great content
- Let links happen organically
- Be patient (takes 3-6 months)
